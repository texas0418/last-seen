# LAST SEEN — Case One: Widow's Point

**⚠️ TOTAL SPOILERS. This is the designer's bible: ground truth, every puzzle
solution, the clue map. Never quote from this file into player-readable
content.**

## Logline

Six days after your estranged sister vanishes from a small fishing town, her
phone arrives in your mailbox with no note. Everyone says she jumped from
Widow's Point. Her phone says otherwise — if you can get past her locks, her
lies, and the people still texting it.

## The franchise

LAST SEEN = the brand (like The Room). Each case is a new found phone: new
owner, new mystery, same engine and doctrine. Case titles: *Last Seen:
Widow's Point*, *Last Seen: <next case>*. The icon ("Status": avatar + dead
presence dot) is the franchise mark.

## Ground truth (what actually happened)

- **~2005–2010** — The sisters' childhood: the fair (2008, "the best day"),
  Ozzy the budgie, and "the game" — their letter-swap cipher. Their father
  crews for Halloway Seafood his whole life.
- **2023** — Dad dies at sea (cardiac event on a double shift Halloway
  should never have scheduled — Quinn's private conviction, never proven).
  The sisters fracture over the funeral. Quinn's last text to Casey, in
  cipher, goes unanswered: `HGROO TZNV?` ("still game?").
- **Apr 2025** — Eli Soto (b. 1991), deckhand on the F/V Dora Mae, dies on a
  dredge detail in shellfish Bed 7. Halloway had lab results ten days
  earlier: the bed was toxic, "worker contact not advised." Sheriff Dunmore
  (Roy Halloway's brother-in-law) signs it off as accidental drowning. Two
  pages of the incident report later go missing; Eli's widow **Rosa Soto**
  photographed them first.
- **May 2026** — Quinn, Halloway's bookkeeper, finds the twin-invoice scheme:
  every real lab invoice (Brennan Analytical) has a same-day same-amount twin
  paid to "Coastal Remediation LLC," a shell that launders the substitution
  of clean test results in the county filings (report **R-1147-B**, the
  toxic Bed 7 result, was replaced by a forged **R-1147-C**). She starts
  copying everything into a hidden account (tidewater.ledger@).
- **Jun–Oct 2026** — She feeds journalist **Mara Reyes** ("M 🌊", texts
  written to read as an affair). Roy's fixer **"T" — Terrence Vale**,
  ex-harbor patrol, now Halloway "security consultant" — surveils and
  threatens her. **Marcus Halloway** (Roy's son) separately suspects his
  father and clumsily asks Quinn about "what you took"; he's been copying
  the Soto records himself. Red-herring villain, actual witness.
- **Oct 11** — T: "last warning." Quinn writes (never sends) the ciphered
  draft to Casey. Marcus texts her he'll testify; she deletes it to protect
  him (recoverable from cloud trash).
- **Oct 12, 5:40 AM** — Car left at Widow's Point (scouted Oct 9 — the one
  uncaptioned photo); she takes the dawn NORTH LINE ferry to Kestrel Bay.
- **Oct 13–14** — From Kestrel Bay: mails the phone (smudged postmark), pays
  cash IN STORE at Tidepool Florals for Mom's lilies ("like the fair. – Q").
  Burner ends 0412.
- **Oct 18** — The phone reaches Casey. Night One begins.

Why staged death: T's escalation plus a sheriff she can't report to. Why the
phone goes to Casey: the evidence had to survive outside her hands, with the
one person she trusts, who can decode her, and whom nobody in Port Brennan
watches. The phone is CURATED — the late-game realization is that everything
the player fought through was Quinn's design.

# THE TWELVE NIGHTS (v2 structure)

Locked 2026-07-26 after playtest 1. Chapters ("Nights") in the diegetic
timeline Oct 18–29; each night ends on a cliff and the next unlocks
IMMEDIATELY (binge model, The Room). The scripted clock runs inside each
night (evening → small hours); the battery arcs down and "charges" between
nights. Nights 1–2 free; one IAP unlocks 3–12 (~$4.99). A finished night
gets a one-line "Previously" recap card in Casey's serif voice.

Principles: Casey has AGENCY (live threads where she asks people to do
things in the world and they report back); the cipher side-content is
optional depth, never gating the spine; every night must hold the doctrine
(≥2 clues, ≥2 surfaces, an inference step, no explaining the trick).

## Night-by-night

**N1 — THE ENVELOPE** *(free)*
Intro, lock screen, first hour with the phone. Teaches: photos zoom, the
badge economy, the live world (T's first NO CALLER ID text lands mid-night).
GATE: passcode `2008` (fair-strip watermark).
Cliff: Mom calls the phone. Casey watches it ring and doesn't answer.

**N2 — UNREAD** *(free; paywall at the cliff)*
The surface of Quinn's life. All three herrings planted and BELIEVED. Dae's
live script: suspicion → grief → alliance ("prove it's really casey" soft
gate: name what's on Quinn's lock screen). The old thread's ciphered
sign-offs itch. Optional: the torn-chart photo can be found early.
Cliff: Dae — "her email got a login attempt yesterday. WHO is trying?"

**N3 — PASSWORD**
The three-surface hunt (banter → bird → Alder Street arithmetic).
GATE: personal mail `ozzy2014`. Inbox fallout: invoices, newsletter,
pharmacy statement (kills herring 3), the storage-full email (plants
tidewater). Cliff: the AP-batch attachment — two invoices, same day, same
amount, different payees.

**N4 — M 🌊**
The affair night. The player should be CERTAIN, then wrong: act-2 M texts
(FOIA slip, "tell eli's wife first"), the newsletter byline, and Mara's
first live contact — she tests before she trusts.
GATE (Mara test 1): `coastal remediation`.
Cliff: Mara — "she wasn't my source on the Soto story. she was my WITNESS."

**N5 — THE BOOKS**
The accounting night: Quinn's actual job becomes the player's. Match invoice
pairs across three PDF attachments, find the county filing discrepancy.
GATE (Mara test 2): which report was swapped — `r1147` (accepts r-1147,
r1147b). Sources: Brennan Analytical invoice memo line + the archived county
filing PDF + the twin-invoice date grid the player assembles by hand.
Cliff: T — "you opened the books. that wasn't yours to do either."

**N6 — MARCUS**
His cold texts, his voicemail, his father's shadow. New app: Calendar
(appointments that contradict the affair AND alibi Marcus). The cloud-trash
restore moves here and gets a gate.
GATE: cloud PIN `0214` (calendar "Mom — Harborview rm 214" + Dae's "her
PINs were always about your mom after the diagnosis"). Restores Marcus's
deleted Oct 11 message: "I know what he did to the Soto reports. I've been
copying them too. I'll testify. — M.H."
Cliff: the message was deleted Oct 11, 11:58 PM — SHE deleted it. Why
protect him?

**N7 — ROSA**
Agency night: Casey texts Eli's widow from Quinn's phone. Trust must be
earned, not assumed — Rosa answers only proof from inside Quinn's world.
GATE (Rosa's test): what's written under Eli's name on the memorial flyer —
`the water doesn't forgive`. Her payback: Eli's last voicemail (he says Bed
7 "smelled wrong" and they were told to keep quiet) + her photos of the two
missing incident-report pages (Dunmore's signature, timestamp altered).
Cliff: Rosa — "your sister called me the night before. she said if anything
happened, the tide book would still be there. what's a tide book?"

**N8 — T**
Who is T? Pattern night. His six weeks of texts get re-read as evidence:
he knew the blue coat, the office hours, the sweater — a watcher with a
schedule. The car photo he sent has a reflection (harbor-patrol jacket); a
months-old port newsletter email announces "Harbor Patrol's T. Vale joins
Halloway Seafood as security consultant."
GATE (Mara needs a name for the story): `vale`.
Also: the airplane-mode sequence, finally real — a scripted 3-beat toggle
dilemma (trace pings vs. a cloud sync finishing), atmosphere-only failure
per doctrine. Cliff: T goes silent for the first time in the game. Silence
reads worse than threats.

**N9 — TIDEWATER**
The cipher-hint login (the chart + XZHVB proof + flyer facts).
GATE: `doramae1991`. The archive night: everything Quinn copied, read in
one sitting. GATE (Mara test 3, from the transmittals): who signed —
`r halloway` (accepts roy halloway). Cliff: the Drafts folder. One unsent
message. It's gibberish.

**N10 — THE FLOWERS**
The alive night. Florist receipt (Oct 14, in store, cash), the ferry photo's
circled dawn sailing, the smudged postmark — and Mom's Oct 15 voicemail
recontextualized from grief-noise to proof.
GATE (Mara: "where was she on the 14th — prove it, don't guess"):
`tidepool florals`. Cliff: Casey texts the flower-card line to Dae; Dae —
"'like the fair.' casey. who else would know what that means?"

**N11 — THE DRAFT**
One night, one cryptogram, alone at 4 AM. The long hand-decode (the decoder
countdown surfaces at ≤5 letters). The draft names the choice and "the old
words" — which are NOT in it. The player must remember, or find, the old
thread: `HGROO TZNV?`, the question Casey never answered.
Cliff: the decode completes; a new thread appears: Unknown · ···-0412.

**N12 — STILL GAME**
GATE: the burner — `still game` (the answer is three years late).
The choice, in-fiction confirms, and the loop that reopens after each
epilogue. GATE (ending 3): the town — `kestrel bay`. Ending 4 (Dunmore's
thread) remains reachable from N7 onward. ENDING N OF 4 chrome; Settings
counts endings seen.
