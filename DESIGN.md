# LAST SEEN — Case One: Widow's Point

**⚠️ TOTAL SPOILERS. This is the designer's bible: ground truth, every puzzle
solution, the clue map. Never quote from this file into player-readable
content.**

## Logline

Six days after your estranged sister vanishes from a small fishing town, her
phone arrives in your mailbox with no note. Everyone says she jumped from
Widow's Point. Her phone says otherwise — if you can get past her locks, her
lies, and the people still texting it.

## The franchise

LAST SEEN = the brand (like The Room). Each case is a new found phone: new
owner, new mystery, same engine and doctrine. Case titles: *Last Seen:
Widow's Point*, *Last Seen: <next case>*. The icon (bubble + typing dots +
one unread badge) is the franchise mark.

## Ground truth (what actually happened)

- **Apr 2025** — Eli Soto (b. 1991), deckhand on the F/V Dora Mae, dies on a
  dredge detail in shellfish Bed 7. Halloway Seafood had received lab results
  ten days earlier: the bed was toxic, "worker contact not advised." Sheriff
  Dunmore (Roy Halloway's brother-in-law) signs it off as accidental drowning.
- **May 2026** — Quinn Mercer, Halloway's bookkeeper, notices every invoice
  from the real lab (Brennan Analytical) has a same-day, same-amount twin
  paid to "Coastal Remediation LLC" — a shell that launders the substitution
  of clean test results. She starts copying everything into a hidden mail
  account (tidewater.ledger@), "the tide book."
- **Jun–Oct 2026** — She feeds journalist Mara Reyes, saved in her phone as
  "M 🌊" with deliberately affair-flavored texts as cover. Roy Halloway's
  fixer ("T") starts surveilling and threatening her. Marcus Halloway (Roy's
  son) separately suspects his father and clumsily asks Quinn about "what you
  took" — he is a red-herring villain, actually a would-be witness.
- **Oct 11** — T: "last warning." Quinn writes (and never sends) a ciphered
  draft to Casey, buys nothing, tells no one.
- **Oct 12, 5:40 AM** — She leaves her car at Widow's Point (scouted Oct 9 —
  the one uncaptioned photo) and takes the dawn NORTH LINE ferry to Kestrel
  Bay. The town concludes suicide.
- **Oct 13–14** — From Kestrel Bay: mails the phone to Casey (smudged
  postmark), pays cash IN STORE at Tidepool Florals for lilies delivered to
  Mom ("like the fair. – Q"). Her prepaid burner ends 0412.
- **Oct 18** — The phone reaches Casey. Game starts.

Why staged death instead of just leaving: T's escalation plus a sheriff she
can't report to. Why mail the phone to Casey: the evidence needed to survive
outside her hands, held by the one person who (a) she trusts, (b) can decode
her, (c) nobody in Port Brennan is watching. The phone is CURATED — the Act 3
realization is that everything the player fought through was Quinn's design.

## Structure

- **Act 1 (free)** — lock screen → the phone's surface. Herrings planted:
  suicide (overlook photo), affair (M 🌊), money/pills trouble (pharmacy).
- **Act 2 (post-mail-password / paywall)** — herrings collapse one by one via
  evidence; Eli Soto surfaces; Mara makes contact (live) and tests the player;
  Mom's Oct 15 voicemail lands the "she's alive?" gut-punch as ambiguity.
- **Act 3 (post-tidewater)** — florist receipt CONFIRMS alive; the curated-
  phone reversal; cloud-restored Marcus message; the ciphered draft; the
  burner; four endings.

Cipher: atbash (A↔Z). Its self-inverse property is why two kids would keep
it. NEVER name it in player-readable text. All ciphertext in content is
generated by `flip()` and verified by test-content.ts.

## The four endings

1. **LOUD** (`ending1`) — send the archive to Mara. Exposé, indictment,
   Quinn can come home eventually.
2. **QUIET** (`ending2`) — honor the draft's other option. Delete it all.
   Annual anonymous postcards.
3. **THE FAIR** (`ending3`) — solve the town, tell the burner. Reunion in
   Kestrel Bay; the evidence choice remains, made together. The "true"
   ending for completionists — hardest to reach.
4. **PROCEDURE** (`ending4`) — give Dunmore the phone. Everything sinks.
   The cautionary ending; reachable from his live thread at any point in Act 3.

## Known deliberate simplifications (v1)

- Photos are described scenes, not images (alt + closer text). Real art is a
  pre-ship item; the closer layer then becomes in-image zoom detail.
- Airplane mode is flavor + Settings toggle; the planned trace-vs-cloud-restore
  timing dilemma is cut from v1. Recovering Marcus's deleted message is a
  one-tap "Cloud trash" restore in Settings once act 3 opens (see README
  pre-ship for the full dilemma design).
- Voicemails are transcript-only ("audio unavailable" is diegetic on a phone
  with 9% battery). Recording Mom's three voicemails is the highest-value
  audio work if we do it.
